Artificial intelligence (AI) is now a major part of our everyday lives, whether
we want it to be or not. It can be found in search engines, smartphones,
customer service chatbots, and various writing and image-generation tools. But
what exactly is AI, when there are so many different AI services and programs
available?

Simply put, AI is a computer program that uses a vast amount of mathematics to
process information and generate, for example, text, images, or other types of
content. AI models are trained in advance using large amounts of data, after
which they are able to answer questions and create new content based on what
they have learned.

Developing AI requires a great deal of computing power. As a result, training
and using large AI models consumes energy and natural resources. For this
reason, the environmental impact of AI is widely discussed today.

One of the best-known AI services is [ChatGPT], which was developed by [OpenAI].
Other well-known companies developing AI include [Anthropic] and [Google], whose
AI service is called [Gemini].
